From Taiwan, in Thailand since 1987

NANPAO began making adhesives in 1963 and launched 815, Taiwan’s first cement paint, in 1964. Today NANPAO is Taiwan’s leading adhesive brand and one of the world’s top three footwear adhesive brands.

Thailand was NANPAO’s first overseas base, and we have operated here since 1987. We apply our R&D and chemical expertise to industry, creating quality products that are easy to use for individual and business customers alike.
